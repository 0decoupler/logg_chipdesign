MOSFET -> or a MOS transistor, Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor = **unlike mechanical switches, they are triggered by a voltage** 
    -nMOS (N-type MOS, where N stands for Negative)
    +pMOS (P-type MOS, where P stands for Positive)

-nMOS => 
    when %V_G - V_S =% **large**, electricity flows between source & drain <-> switch **closed**
    when %V_G - V_S =% **small**, electricity !flow between source & drain <-> switch **open**
+pMOS => opposite of -nMOS

CMOS -> nMOS and pMOS (Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor)

Digital Signal Processing -> logic gates out of 1 and 0 defined transistors --> CMOS circuits to convert those 0 and 1s.
